# Introduction

Sequencing technologies have become an important part of modern healthcare because they allow researchers and doctors to examine genetic information in much greater detail.

One company that has built its business around sequencing and genomic information is **Tempus AI**. The company combines genetic testing, clinical information, artificial intelligence, and large-scale data analysis to support more personalized healthcare.

Tempus began with a strong focus on cancer care and has expanded into a broader healthcare technology company.

---

## <span id="founder">Who founded Tempus?</span>

Tempus was founded by **Eric Lefkofsky** in **2015**.

Lefkofsky became interested in using technology and genomic information to improve cancer treatment after his wife was diagnosed with breast cancer.

The company initially focused heavily on oncology and used next-generation sequencing to connect molecular information with clinical information.

Today, Tempus has expanded beyond its original cancer-focused business into a larger precision-medicine platform.

## <span id="investment">How much was invested?</span>

Tempus received significant investment as it developed its sequencing technology, data infrastructure, and healthcare platform.

The company reported that it had raised approximately **$130 million in total funding by September 2017**.

By 2019, the company had raised approximately **$520 million**, and by December 2020 it had reported approximately **$1.05 billion** in total funding.

These investments helped Tempus develop its genetic testing capabilities, artificial intelligence systems, clinical data infrastructure, and relationships with healthcare and pharmaceutical companies.

> **Important:** There is not one clearly reported public figure for the exact amount invested on the day Tempus was founded. The figures above refer to funding raised during the company's development rather than a single initial investment.

## <span id="revenue">What is Tempus's current revenue?</span>

Tempus has grown substantially since its founding.

According to the company's 2025 financial results, Tempus reported approximately **$1.27 billion in total revenue for 2025**.

Its business is divided into two major areas:

- **Diagnostics**
- **Data and Applications**

In 2025, diagnostics generated approximately **$955 million**, while data and applications generated approximately **$316 million**.

The diagnostics business includes genetic testing, while the data and applications business provides information and analytical tools to healthcare and pharmaceutical companies.

### Why is revenue important?

Tempus's revenue demonstrates how sequencing technology can become part of a larger business model.

Rather than only selling sequencing as a laboratory service, Tempus uses the information generated through testing as part of a broader platform involving healthcare data, artificial intelligence, and pharmaceutical research.

## <span id="business">What is Tempus's main business focus?</span>

Tempus's primary focus is **precision medicine**.

Precision medicine attempts to use information about an individual patient to help healthcare professionals make more informed decisions about treatment.

Tempus combines several types of information, including:

- Genetic sequencing
- Clinical records
- Molecular information
- Medical imaging
- Artificial intelligence
- Healthcare data

### Cancer treatment

Cancer is one of Tempus's major areas of focus.

Genetic testing can identify mutations and other molecular characteristics of a patient's tumor.

Doctors can use this information alongside other clinical information when considering treatment options.

This is particularly important because cancers can differ at the molecular level even when they occur in the same organ.

## <span id="sequencing">How does sequencing fit into the business?</span>

Sequencing is an important part of Tempus's technology platform.

Next-generation sequencing allows DNA and other molecular information to be examined from patient samples.

Tempus combines sequencing results with clinical information to create a more comprehensive view of a patient's disease.

The company can then use this information in several ways.

### 1. Patient care

Genomic information can provide doctors with additional information about a patient's disease.

### 2. Pharmaceutical research

Pharmaceutical companies can use genomic and clinical datasets to support drug discovery and development.

### 3. Clinical trials

Genomic and clinical information can help researchers identify patients who may qualify for certain clinical trials.

### 4. Data analysis

Tempus can use artificial intelligence and data-analysis tools to identify patterns across large healthcare datasets.

This creates a business model where the same underlying technology can support multiple types of customers.

## <span id="future">What is the future of Tempus?</span>

The future of Tempus will depend on several factors.

One is the continued development of **precision medicine**. As genomic testing becomes more integrated into healthcare, companies that can connect genetic information with clinical data may have opportunities to expand.

Another important area is **artificial intelligence**.

Tempus is developing AI systems that can analyze large amounts of medical, genomic, and imaging information.

The company is also expanding its relationships with pharmaceutical companies. During 2025, Tempus reported signing data agreements with more than **70 biopharmaceutical customers**.

These developments suggest that Tempus is attempting to build a business that extends beyond genetic testing and into a broader healthcare data and technology platform.

### Challenges

However, future growth is not guaranteed.

Tempus operates in healthcare, where companies must deal with:

- Healthcare regulations
- Data privacy
- Competition
- Reimbursement
- Medical research requirements
- The cost of developing new technologies

The company also needs to demonstrate that its genetic testing, data products, and AI technologies provide useful results for doctors, patients, researchers, and pharmaceutical companies.

Therefore, Tempus's future success will depend on how effectively it can turn sequencing and healthcare data into useful and sustainable products.
